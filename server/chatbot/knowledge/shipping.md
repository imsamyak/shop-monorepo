# Shipping

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $9.99.

Shipping is free on orders over $50. Orders under $50 pay a flat $4.99 standard shipping fee.

Orders placed before 2 PM on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends, ship the next business day.

We currently ship within the country only. International shipping is not available yet.

Once an order ships you receive an email with a tracking link. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show updates.

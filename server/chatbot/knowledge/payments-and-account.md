# Payments

We accept credit and debit cards, UPI, and cash on delivery for orders under ₹15,000.

Your card is charged when you place the order. If an item is out of stock after ordering, that item is refunded automatically.

# Account

To create an account, click "Sign up" and register with your email and a password.

Forgot your password? Click "Forgot password" on the login page and we email you a reset link. The link expires after 30 minutes.

You can become a seller by contacting the admin team. Seller accounts can list products and manage stock.
